What is a transistor?

A transistor is a three-terminal semiconductor device used for amplification, switching and signal control. In this studio, the two main families are BJTs and MOSFETs.

  • BJT terminals: base, collector, emitter.
  • MOSFET terminals: gate, drain, source, body.
  • The useful question is always: which voltage controls which current?

BJT regions

For an NPN transistor, a simplified teaching model is enough to start.

  • Cutoff: base drive is too small, collector current is near zero.
  • Active: collector current is approximately β times base current.
  • Saturation: the transistor is driven hard on and VCE becomes small.
IC ≈ βIB in active-region analysis.

MOSFET regions

For an enhancement NMOS, the gate controls the channel without requiring a steady DC gate current in the idealized model.

  • Cutoff: VGS below threshold.
  • Ohmic or triode: device behaves like a voltage-controlled channel.
  • Saturation: current is primarily controlled by overdrive VGS − VTH in the square-law model.

Biasing first, gain second

An amplifier needs a stable operating point. A useful design sequence is DC bias first, then small-signal gain, then swing and power limits.

Q-point → gm or re → gain → swing → power

Why the load line matters

The resistor network limits how much current and voltage the transistor can have at the same time. The intersection with the transistor characteristic is the operating point.

Why real devices differ

Datasheet values depend on temperature, device batch, current, voltage, package and test conditions. Calculator results here are first-pass design estimates.

Formula Reference

BJT DC

Fixed bias: IB = (VCC − VBE)/RB.

Active region: IC ≈ βIB.

Divider bias: IB = (VTH − VBE)/(RTH + (β + 1)RE).

VCE = VCC − ICRC − IERE.

MOSFET DC

Overdrive: VOV = VGS − VTH.

Square-law saturation: ID ≈ 0.5K(VOV)².

Ohmic condition: VDS < VOV.

Saturation condition: VDS ≥ VOV.

BJT small signal

gm = IC/VT.

rπ = β/gm.

re ≈ VT/IE ≈ 1/gm for a first-pass estimate.

CE gain with bypassed emitter: Av ≈ −gm(RC || RL).

MOSFET small signal

gm ≈ √(2KID) using the K convention in this app.

Common-source gain with optional ro: Av ≈ −gm(RD || RL || ro).

Switch conduction loss: P ≈ ID²RDS(on).
